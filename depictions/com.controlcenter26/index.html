ControlCenter26

com.controlcenter26 · 1.0

Description

A Control Center similar to iOS 26.

ControlCenter26 reshapes Control Center to look and behave like the one in iOS 26: resizable and reorderable modules, connectivity buttons, media and page controls, and smoother animations.

Settings ▸ Control Center holds the options.

Packages for rootless (iphoneos-arm64) and roothide (iphoneos-arm64e) jailbreaks. Requires iOS 14.0 to 17.3, ellekit or MobileSubstrate, CCSupport (com.opa334.ccsupport) and PreferenceLoader.
